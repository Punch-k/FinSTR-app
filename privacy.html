Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 · Terms of Use

FinSTR is a free, open-source research tool for S&P 500 stocks. You can use all of it without an account. This policy covers what happens if you choose to sign in.

Without an account

Your paper portfolio, watchlist and screen alerts are saved only in your own browser (local storage). FinSTR has no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and does not set tracking cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you sign in

What we storeWhy
Your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by GoogleTo identify your account and send the sign-in link or the digests you switch on
The paper portfolio, watchlist, screen alerts and email preferences you saveTo keep them in sync across your devices
Sign-in timestamps and technical session data kept by our authentication providerTo keep your account secure

We request only basic Google profile access (email, name, picture). We never see or store your Google password, and we do not access any other Google data such as Gmail, Drive or contacts.

How it is used

  • Only to provide FinSTR's features: sign-in, sync, and the emails you ask for (for example a weekly portfolio digest).
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data, and do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not send marketing emails. Digest emails are sent only if you switch them on, and every digest explains how to stop it.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Account and portfolio data are stored with Supabase (database and authentication) in the United States. Each account can read and change only its own data, enforced by database row-level security. Emails are delivered through Resend.

Your choices

  • Delete your saved data: sign in, open your account (top right), and choose "Delete my saved data". This removes your portfolio, watchlist, screen alerts and preferences from our database immediately.
  • Close your account entirely (including your email address): open an issue on GitHub or reply to any FinSTR email, and it will be removed within 30 days.
  • You can revoke FinSTR's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

FinSTR is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The source of this page is public in the FinSTR repository, so every change is visible.
