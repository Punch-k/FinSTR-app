      About FinSTR

      A Finviz-style stock screener for the S&P 500, built by Prapanch Kokkalemada.

      What it does

      • Screener: filter and sort all S&P 500 companies across valuation, financial, ownership, performance, technical and analyst views; export CSV; share any view by link.
      • Maps: market-cap treemap coloured by 1-day to 1-year performance.
      • Quote panel: click any stock for a price chart with 50/200-day averages, a 70-field snapshot, company profile, headlines, analyst ratings and insider trading.
      • Markets: index, futures, forex and crypto snapshots; sector and industry groups; insider trades; earnings calendar.

      Data

      Method notes

      • Performance, moving averages, RSI (14, Wilder), volatility (std. dev. of daily returns), ATR and 52-week levels are computed from daily closes, the same way for every stock.
      • Valuation, margins, ownership, short interest and analyst targets are Yahoo Finance's reported figures. A missing value shows as "-", never as zero.
      • The FinSTR Signal ranks every stock 0-100 against the S&P 500 on six factors (trend, momentum, value, quality, growth, low risk). Its price-based factors are backtested monthly and the results, good or bad, are on the Signals page. It is not investment advice.
      • Relative volume is pace-adjusted during the session: today's volume so far is compared with the share of a normal day's volume that has usually traded by that time.
      • "Why it moved" notes (moves of 3% or more) check three things: an earnings report in the last trading day, how the stock's industry moved, and the latest headline that names the company. They describe; they do not claim a cause.
      • The Live record on the Signals page saves the strongest and weakest fifth every trading day and measures them afterwards, so it is out-of-sample by construction.
      • Data refreshes several times each US trading day. It is a snapshot, not a live feed.

      Built with

      Python data pipeline (yfinance, pandas) on a scheduled GitHub Action · static site with D3 and TradingView Lightweight Charts · no server, no tracking.
