    About FinSTR

    A Finviz-style stock screener for the S&P 500, built by Prapanch Kokkalemada.

    What it does

    • Screener: filter and sort all S&P 500 companies across valuation, financial, ownership, performance, technical and analyst views; export CSV; share any view by link.
    • Maps: market-cap treemap coloured by 1-day to 1-year performance.
    • Quote panel: click any stock for a price chart with 50/200-day averages, a 70-field snapshot, company profile, headlines and a valuation sandbox.
    • Markets: index, futures, forex and crypto snapshots; sector and industry groups; insider trades; earnings calendar.

    Data

    Method notes

    • Performance, moving averages, RSI (14, Wilder), volatility (std. dev. of daily returns), ATR and 52-week levels are computed from daily closes, the same way for every stock.
    • Valuation, margins, ownership, short interest and analyst targets are Yahoo Finance's reported figures. A missing value shows as "-", never as zero.
    • The FinSTR Score is a simple 0-100 blend of a value score (P/E, PEG) and a momentum score. The valuation sandbox uses your own growth and discount assumptions. Neither is investment advice.
    • Data refreshes several times each US trading day. It is a snapshot, not a live feed.

    Built with

    Python data pipeline (yfinance, pandas) on a scheduled GitHub Action · static site with D3 and TradingView Lightweight Charts · no server, no tracking.
